# Definition
- Extrusion of bowel through abdominal wall
- As opposed to [Omphalocele](Gastroenterology/GI%20Pathologies/Omphalocele.md)

# Etiology
- Probably involves incomplete closure of abdominal wall, paraumbilical abdominal wall defect

# Clinical features
- Usually on right side of umbilical cord
- Not covered by peritoneum
- Poor GI function, usually associated with atresia, stenosis 
- Few associated defects, if GI function restored, good prognosis
- Rarely associated with Down, other congenital disease
# Treatment 
- Surgery
