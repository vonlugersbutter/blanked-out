# Definition
Inflammation of the liver, basically.
- Liver fibrosis = fibroblast proliferation, putting down ECM components, like collagen when liver injury happens
- [Cirrhosis](Gastroenterology/GI%20Disorders/Cirrhosis.md) is the final stage of chronic liver disease 
# Etiology
## Alcohol-associated hepatitis 
- Alcoholic liver disease = progresses from alcoholic fatty liver (asymptomatic, reversible accumulation of fatty acids) to acute hepatitis and eventually cirrhosis
- Alcoholic hepatitis = caused by acetaldehyde toxicity; presents with fever, jaundice, and RUQ pain biopsy shows Mallory bodies (damaged intermediate filaments)
### Risk factors
- History of chronic heavy alcohol use
- Cigarette smoking
- Genetics
- Female sex → even though men are more likely to drink excessively, women are more susceptible towards disease states because of differences in alcohol metabolism
- Obesity, malnutrition 
## Other causes 
- Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) = associated with obesity; presents with ALT > AST and can progress from simple fat (NAFL) to inflammation (NASH)
- Liver zones = zone 3 (centrilobular) is where alcoholic fatty infiltration begins and is the primary site of damage in shock liver (ischemic hepatitis) and Budd-Chiari syndrome (hepatic vein thrombosis)
- Budd-Chiari syndrome = presents with abdominal pain, ascites, and hepatomegaly, often due to myeloproliferative disorders or pregnancy
- Right heart failure = causes "cardiac cirrhosis" and a nutmeg liver appearance
- Reye’s syndrome = occurs in children using aspirin for viral infections; it inhibits beta-oxidation and causes severe mitochondrial damage and liver failure
- α1 anti-trypsin deficiency = an autosomal co-dominant disorder where abnormal aat polymerizes in the endoplasmic reticulum of hepatocytes, causing cirrhosis these polymers stain with pas and resist diastase digestion
	- Also implicated in [COPD](Respirology/Respiratory%20Pathologies/COPD.md)
- Liver abscess = in the US, these are usually bacterial (from bacteremia or cholangitis); globally, parasites like Entameba histolytica or Echinococcus are common
- Autoimmune hepatitis = usually affects women in their 40s/50s and is characterized by anti-nuclear antibodies (ANA) and anti-smooth muscle antibodies (ASMA)
- Tylenol (acetaminophen) overdose = causes acute liver failure with extremely high AST/ALT the toxic metabolite NAPQI is normally metabolized by glutathione treatment is n-acetylcysteine, which replenishes glutathione
# Epidemiology
# Clinical features
- Definition of alcohol-associated hepatitis!
	- Onset of jaundice within the past 8 weeks
	- >6 months of ongoing alcohol consumption above threshold, <60 continuous days of abstinence from alcohol 
- Jaundice, fever, leukocytosis, tender hepatomegaly, fatigue fever, weight loss → all symptoms of acute hepatitis 
- Alcohol withdrawal
	- Tremor, agitation, tachycardia, seizures, delirium
- Long-term alcohol use
	- Dupuytren contracture, sarcopenia
# Investigations
- AST and ALT = AST is in mitochondria; alcohol is a mitochondrial toxin, so AST > ALT is classic for alcoholic hepatitis alt is cytoplasmic, and ALT > AST is seen in most other types of hepatitis
- Alk phos = increases with obstructed bile flow (cholestasis), but also rises in pregnancy, thyroid, and bone disease
- GGT = elevated after heavy alcohol use; because it is not elevated in bone disease, it helps determine if a high alk phos is hepatobiliary in origin
- Synthetic function = severe liver disease is indicated by low albumin, low glucose (loss of gluconeogenesis/glycogen breakdown), and elevated pt/ptt (loss of clotting factors)
- Imaging
	- RUQ with Doppler to show fatty liver disease or cirrhosis
	- CT abdomen 
- Liver biopsy if the diagnosis is unclear because of confounding factors → transjugular
# Treatment 
- Immediate alcohol cessation, ensure adequate caloric intake, use MELD score 
- For alcohol-induced, use naltrexone or disulfram to limit alcohol use
- For acetaminophen overdose, n-acetylcysteine is used to counteract the production of NAQPI 
- Early liver transplantation may have to occur 
# Prognosis
- Can eventually progress to [Cirrhosis](Gastroenterology/GI%20Disorders/Cirrhosis.md)
