# Blood tests
- Blood urea nitrogen = measures the blood nitrogen that is part of urea resulting from deamination of AAs 
- BUN rise means that GFR is low 
- Plasma creatinine = metabolism of creatine phosphate in skeletal muscle, normally steady because it gets discharged in the urine
- Renal plasma clearance 
$$
clearance = \frac{U \times V}{P}
$$
- Inulin, for example, is a plant polysaccharide that is easily filtered out but not reabsorbed, and is used as a proxy for the GFR 
- Para-amino hippuric acid (PAH) → measured renal plasma flow
