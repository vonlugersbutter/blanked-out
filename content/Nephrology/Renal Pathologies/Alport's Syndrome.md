# Definition
Hematuria, progressive glomerulonephritis, possible end stage renal disease. Mutations in type IV collagen, which comprises a lot of the glomerular basement membrane. Becomes irregular in thickness, cannot be a good filtration barrier to RBCs and proteins. Autosomal recessive.
