# Etiology
- Type A (autoimmune) = involves autoimmune destruction of parietal cells in the gastric body and fundus it leads to pernicious anemia due to the loss of intrinsic factor (required for b12 absorption) it is associated with HLA-DR antigens and an increased risk of adenocarcinoma
- Type B (bacteria) = caused by h pylori, a gram-negative rod that typically infects the antrum
	- Mechanisms = it is urease-positive, producing ammonium to protect itself from acid this increases gastric pH, triggering gastrin release and further acid production
	- Malignancies = highly associated with [MALT lymphoma](Gastroenterology/GI%20Pathologies/MALT%20lymphoma.md) (b-cell cancer) and gastric adenocarcinoma
	- Diagnosis and treatment = diagnosed via biopsy, urea breath test, or stool antigen treated with "triple therapy" (PPI, clarithromycin, and amoxicillin/metronidazole)
- Metaplastic atrophic gastritis = chronic inflammation can lead to intestinal metaplasia, where goblet cells appear in the stomach tissue
