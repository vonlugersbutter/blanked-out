# Some genetic notes from Lilly
- Down syndrome (trisomy 21) = really common to have congenital heart defects
- Abnormal migration patterns of endocardial cushion migration
- Turner syndrome (45, X0) = left-sided obstructive lesions
- Williams syndrome = issues with aorta and pulmonary artery obstruction
	- Deletion in chromosome 7
	- Elastin gene, needed for arteries
- DiGeorge syndrome = pharyngeal defects, hypocalcaemia due to absent parathyroid glands, and T-cell dysfunction secondary to hypoplasia of the thymus
	- Microdeletion in chromosome 22
	- Needed in cardiac outflow tracts
