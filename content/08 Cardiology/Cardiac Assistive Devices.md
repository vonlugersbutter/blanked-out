| Technology or technique       | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cardiac transplant            | Needs to take immunosuppressants for lifetime. Vagus nerve is severed, so new heart beats at 100 bpm.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Intra-aortic balloon pump     | Temporary mechanical device inserted into the aorta to help a weakened heart pump blood more effectively. It works by inflating a balloon during diastole (when the heart rests) to increase blood flow and coronary artery perfusion, and rapidly deflating before systole (the next heart beat) to reduce the heart's workload. |
| Ventricular assist device     | Mechanical device, helps the ventricle pump.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Cardiomyoplasty               | Take some skeletal muscle, wrap it around the heart, make it pump with a pacemaker.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Skeletal muscle assist device | Skeletal muscle made into a pouch, placed between aorta and heart to function as a booster heart, make it pump with a pacemaker.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
