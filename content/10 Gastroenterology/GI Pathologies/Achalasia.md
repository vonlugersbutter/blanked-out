# Definition
Failure of the LES to relax due to a lack of nitric oxide synthase and VIP in inhibitory nerves
# Etiology
- The inability of the les to relax due to the loss of ganglion cells in Auerbach’s plexus
- Causes dysphagia to both solids and liquids and may cause bad breath from food accumulation
