# Etiology
- Adenocarcinoma = this is the most common type in the us, typically developing in the lower 1/3 of the esophagus near the stomach acid it results from the progression of GERD to Barrett’s, with obesity being a major risk factor
- Squamous cell carcinoma = most common worldwide, it usually occurs in the middle or upper esophagus risks include alcohol, hot tea, achalasia, esophageal webs, Zenker’s, and lye ingestion
- Lymph node spread = upper tumors spread to cervical nodes; middle to mediastinal/tracheobronchial nodes; and lower to celiac/gastric nodes
