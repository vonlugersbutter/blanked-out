# Ureters
- Peristaltic contractions of muscular ureters push towards bladder 
- No anatomic valve at the opening of the ureters
- When bladder fills → pressure compresses oblique openings → prevents backflow of urine
- Three layers of tissue for the ureters
	- Mucosa with transitional epithelium and lamina propria of areolar connective tissue 
	- Muscularis → inner longitudinal and outer circular layers of smooth muscle
		- The distal third of the ureters also has an additional outer longitudinal layer of muscles
	- Adventitia → layer of connective tissue with blood vessels, lymphatics, and nerves 
# Bladder

- Trigone = two ureteral openings and opening into internal urethral orifice 
- Three coats 
	- Mucosa (transitional epithelium and lamina propria)
	- Muscularis or detrusor muscle (with inner longitudinal, middle circular, outer longitudinal)
	- Adventitia
- Internal urethral sphincter of circular smooth muscle fibers
- External urethral sphincter = skeletal muscle 
- Volume in bladder high → stretch receptors to the spinal cord → micturition center in sacral S2 and S3 → PNS impulses cause contraction of detrusor muscle and relaxation of the internal urethral sphincter muscle and inhibition of somatic motor neurons of the external sphincter 
- Male urethra = prostatic, intermedia, spongy
