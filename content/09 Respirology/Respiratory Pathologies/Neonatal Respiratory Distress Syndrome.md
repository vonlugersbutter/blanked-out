# Definition
- Hyaline membrane disease of the newborn and infant respiratory distress syndrome 
- Several features shared with [ARDS](09%20Respirology/Respiratory%20Pathologies/ARDS.md) 
# Etiology
- Usually because of absence of pulmonary surfactant → this starts to be produced around the 20th week of gestation, and sufficient quantities are not present until much later
# Epidemiology
# Prognosis
- Survivors may develop bronchopulmonary dysplasia → chronic lung disease, decrease in size and number of alveoli + persistent need for supplemental oxygen
# Investigations
- Measure surfactant using lecithin/sphingomyelin ratio of amniotic fluid 
# Clinical features
- Shows hemorrhagic edema, patchy atelectasis, hyaline membranes caused by proteinaceous fluid and cellular debris within the alveoli 
- Profound hypoxemia, V/Q inequality and blood flow through the unventilated lung 
- Even MORE shunting, because the patent foramen ovale 
# Treatment
- Maturation of the surfactant-synthesizing system can be done by administration of corticosteroids to pregnant women delivering at <34 weeks of gestation 
- Exogenous surfactant, nasal continuous positive airway pressure, invasive mechanical ventilation
