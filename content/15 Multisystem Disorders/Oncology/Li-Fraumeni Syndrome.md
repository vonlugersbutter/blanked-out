# Definition
Syndrome of multiple malignancies at an early age.
# Etiology
- Sarcoma, Breast, Leukemia, Adrenal Gland (SBLA) cancer syndrome
- Germline mutation in tumor suppressor gene TP53, which codes for tumor protein p53; delays cell cycle progression to allow for DNA repair
	- See [Tumor Suppressor Genes](Tumor%20Suppressor%20Genes.md)
