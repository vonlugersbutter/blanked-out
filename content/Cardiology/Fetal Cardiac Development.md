We start with Tortora. 
![](00%20media/{DD06D510-24CA-4C03-8AD7-9D02F774145E}.png)
Forms from cardiogenic area in the mesoderm. Cardiogenic cords, then endocardial tubes. Primitive heart tube then turns into the following structures, and what they eventually turn into. Listed from superior to inferior. 

| Structure           | Eventual part of organs                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Truncus arteriosus  | Ascending aorta, pulmonary trunk                                                         |
| Bulbus cordis       | Right ventricle                                                                          |
| Primitive ventricle | Left ventricle                                                                           |
| Primitive atrium    | Anterior wall of right atrium, right auricle, anterior wall of left atrium, left auricle |
| Sinus venosus       | Posterior wall of right atrium, coronary sinus, SA node                                  |
![300](00%20media/{039E5D0C-82E1-42EC-B2EC-E8D1A6CAF6F3}.png)

Endocardial cushions appear, fuse into the atrioventricular canals between atria and ventricles → these divide into left and right. Interatrial septum moves, fuses with endocardial cushions, form an opening called foramen ovale between left and right atria.
