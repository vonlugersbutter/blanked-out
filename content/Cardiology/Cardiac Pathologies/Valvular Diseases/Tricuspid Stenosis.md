# Clinical features
- OS and diastolic murmur of TS sounds like MS, but better at the sternum
- Intensifies on inspiration because ↑ blood flow to right heart 
- Neck veins distended
- Large a waves because of right atrial contraction against stenotic valve
- Abdominal distension and hepatomegaly due to passive venous congestion
# Treatment 
- Percutaneous balloon dilatation or surgical correction
