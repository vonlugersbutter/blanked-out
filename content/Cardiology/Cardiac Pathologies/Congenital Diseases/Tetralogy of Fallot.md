# Definition
Four congenital heart defects:
1. Interventricular septal defect
2. Aorta that emerges from both ventricles instead of just LV (overriding aorta)
3. Stenosed pulmonary valve
4. Enlarged right ventricle
# Etiology
- Caused by abnormal displacement of the outflow tract of the IV septum 
- Related to microdeletion in chromosome 22 
- Pulmonic stenosis → blood from systemic veins goes to LV instead → systemic hypoxemia and cyanosis 
# Epidemiology
# Prognosis
- 5 of 10 000 live births 
- Usually associated with other cardiac defects 
# Investigations
## Physical exam
- Cyanosis
- Clubbing 
- RV hypertrophy as heave along left sternal border 
- Pulmonary component soft and inaudible 
- Systolic ejection murmur through stenotic RV outflow tract 
## CXR
- Prominence of RV, boot shaped heart 
## Echo
- Will visualize all characteristics 
# Clinical features
- Dyspnea on exertion
- Spells following exertion, feeding, crying
- Irritability, cyanosis, hyperventilation, syncope, convulsions 
- Children will squat down, increase SVR by kinking the femoral arteries, decreasing right to left shunt, directing more blood from RV to lungs 
# Treatment 
- Complete surgical repair 
- Antibiotic prophylaxis to prevent endocarditis
