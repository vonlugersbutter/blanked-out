# Investigations
- Fasting gastrin >10x normal and the secretin test (secretin stimulates gastrinomas but inhibits normal g cells)

- Abdominal pain (improves with food), ulcers (often distal), and chronic diarrhea (low pH inactivates pancreatic enzymes)
