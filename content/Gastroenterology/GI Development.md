# Gastric acid
- Birth has very low gastric acid production, pH is 7 or 8, and drops to less than 3 in the first couple of hours
# LES
- Often underdeveloped in infants = common spit-up (reflux) because the muscle isn't fully mature
- Well-developed by around 2 weeks 
# Pancreas
- Exocrine pancreas function isn’t really good until first year
# Intestines
- Babies can absorb intact proteins → including antibodies from breast milk
## Meconium
- Meconium is the earliest stool of an infant resulting from defecation. Unlike later feces, meconium is composed of materials ingested during the time the infant spends in the uterus: intestinal epithelial cells, lanugo, mucus, amniotic fluid, bile, and water
- Unlike later feces, is viscous and sticky like tar – its color usually being a very dark olive green and it is almost odorless
