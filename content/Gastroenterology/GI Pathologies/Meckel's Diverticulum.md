# Definition
- Most common congenital GI abnormality, caused by a persistent remnant of the vitelline duct.
- It is a "true diverticulum" containing all bowel layers and often includes ectopic gastric tissue, which may secrete acid and cause ulceration, pain, or bleeding.
# Etiology
# Epidemiology
Rule of 2’s: 2% of the population, 2:1 male-to-female ratio, within 2 feet of the ileocecal valve, and usually 2 inches in size.
# Prognosis
# Investigations
- Technetium scan taken up by gastric cells in diverticulum 
# Clinical features
- Other anomalies include vitelline cysts, sinuses, or a persistent duct that causes intestinal discharge from the umbilicus.
# Treatment 
- Surgery
