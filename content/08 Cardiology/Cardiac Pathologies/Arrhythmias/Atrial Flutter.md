- 180 to 250 bpm
- Hitting AV node during refractory period, not going to the ventricles
- Slow or normal ventricular rate, usually limited by a ratio
	- For example, if atrial is 300 bpm → 2:1 block → ventricle rate will be 150
- Vagal maneuvers can ↓ AV conduction → ↓ ventricular rate → allow for better atrial visualization on an EKG
- Circles around in the RA, leading to a sawtooth pattern
- Happens in old people, especially those who have had cardiac surgery or prior ablation
- Slowing down the atrial rate may actually worsen the flutter because it is now at a rate that the AV node can transmit to the ventricles, so don't do that
- Atrial thrombus formation is also a risk

# Treatment
- Electrical cardioversion
- Terminated by rapid atrial stimulation (burst pacing from a pacemaker)
- Drugs
	- AV block ↑ : beta blockers, Ca++ channel blockers, digoxin
	- Restore sinus rhythm using antiarrhythmics
- Could also try catheter ablation through the femoral vein and IVC to try to prevent all those circulating impulses
