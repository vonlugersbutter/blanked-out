# Definition
Aka aortic insufficiency.
# Etiology

![300](00%20media/{3A54B241-8350-40BE-9F99-DFA551DE2704}.png)
- Valvular causes
	- Bicuspid aortic valve 
	- Endocarditis (perforation or erosion of leaflet)
	- Rheumatic valve disease
- Primary aortic root disease  = aortic annulus dilates enough to cause separation
	- Age-related degeneration, aortic aneurysm, aortic dissection 

- LV must pump regurgitant volume plus normal quantity 
- Factors affecting severity
	- Size of regurgitant orifice
	- Pressure gradient during diastole
	- Diastolic duration 
- Acute AR
	- LV normal, noncompliant 
	- LV diastolic pressure ↑ → LA and pulmonary circulation pressure ↑ → dyspnea and pulmonary edema 
- Chronic AR
	- Compensatory adaption of LV → eccentric hypertrophy → aortic pressure will drop
	- High LV stroke volume plus ↓ aortic diastolic pressure will produce widened pulse pressure 
	- CPP ↓ → ↓ myocardial oxygen supply 
	- LV size and wall stress → ↑ myocardial oxygen demand 
	- Angina even in the absence of atherosclerosis 
	- Progressive remodeling of LV → systolic dysfunction → ↓ forward CO → ↑ LA and pulmonary pressures → heart failure

# Clinical features
- Dyspnea on exertion
- Fatigue
- Uncomfortable forceful heartbeat because ↑ pulse pressure 
- Bounding pulses
- Pulmonary edema if it is acute AR → bibasilar crackles 
![](00%20media/{D49C3DE1-F0DF-4B77-8B63-0E87237097F7}.png)

- Blowing murmur in early diastole along LLSB
- Low-frequency mid-diastolic rumble at apex in severe AR → Austin Flint murmur
	- Turbulence of flow through mitral valve during diastole due to downward displacement of mitral anterior leaflet by the regurgitant stream of AR
	- Does not have OS or presystolic accentuation like a MS murmur 
# Treatment 
- Clinical progression is slow, use echocardiograms to monitor
- Afterload reducing vasodilators (Ca++ channel blockers, angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor) to reduce hypertension
- Surgical AVR 
- Without surgery, death in 4 years after the development of angina or 2 years after the onset of heart failure
