# Definition
- Trisomy 21
# Etiology
- 95% cases due to nondisjunction 
- Maternal meiosis I NDJ errors are a common cause; meiosis I protracted in females
- Begins prenatally, completed at ovulation years later
- Advanced maternal age → ↑ risk trisomy
- Robertsonian translocation
	- Extra, fused part of chromosome 21 attaches to another chromosome (often 14), causing Down syndrome in about 3-4% of cases
	- Usually a carrier parent with fused chromosome, and child is euploid, but there are three copies of chromosome 21
