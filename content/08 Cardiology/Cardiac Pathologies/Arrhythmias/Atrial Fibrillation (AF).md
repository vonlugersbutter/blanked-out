Common, usually affects older adults. Contraction of atrial fibers is asynchronous, so atrial pumping just stops. 

- Atrial 350-600 bpm waves
- No P waves
- Irregularly irregular rhythm
- Ventricular rate is 140-160 bpm
- Multiple re-entry circuits
- Could be paroxysmal (sudden, unpredictable)
	- This is started in the foci by sleeves of atrial muscles that extend into pulmonary veins
- The greater the atrial size, the more likely the re-entrant circuits exist 
- Things that make this more dangerous
	- Rapid ventricular rates → ↓ CO, especially if already stiff due to impaired filling because beats are just happening so fast
	- Thrombus formation in LA, goes straight to the brain. Do an echo to make sure this isn't happening

# Treatment
- Ventricular rate control
- Restore sinus rhythm
- Anticoagulants

- Could also try some beta blockers, Ca++ channel antagonists, cardioversion
- Maze procedure = surgical incisions in LA and RA to ↓ re-entry circuits
- Percutaneous catheter ablation, again to destroy re-entry pathways
- Catheter ablation of AV node, and then just use a pacemaker to set the ventricular rate 
- Anti-arrhythmic drugs here can be quite dangerous so try to avoid
