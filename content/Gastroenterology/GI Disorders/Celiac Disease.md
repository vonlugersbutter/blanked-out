# Definition
Autoimmune destruction of small intestinal villi (primarily in the duodenum) triggered by gluten exposure.
# Etiology
- The protein gliadin is deamidated by tissue transglutaminase (ttg) APCs present this to t-cells (type iv hypersensitivity) it is strongly associated with hla-dq2 and hla-dq8
# Epidemiology

# Clinical features
- Histology features include blunting of villi, crypt hyperplasia, and lymphocytes in the lamina propria
# Investigations
- Screening involves testing for IgA ttg or anti-endomysial antibodies; a duodenal biopsy confirms the diagnosis
# Prognosis
- Includes a small risk of EATL (t-cell lymphoma) and dermatitis herpetiformis, a herpes-like skin condition that resolves with a gluten-free diet
