# Definition
Conditions where net acid excretion by the kidneys is impaired. Decreased RNAE, acidosis. 

Proximal RTA = defect in H+ secretion by the PCT
- Cystinosis = rare, inherited genetic disorder that causes the amino acid cystine to build up in cells → impaired reabsorption of HCO3-
- Fanconi syndrome = rare kidney disorder where the proximal tubules fail to reabsorb essential nutrients
- Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors 

Distal RTA = DCT, or inadequate production and excretion of NH4+
- Medullary sponge kidney
- Amphotericin B
- Conditions associated with urinary obstruction
