Originates in the ventricles, four or more ventricular premature contraction. Ventricles beat too fast, and this can lead to [Ventricular Fibrillation (VF or V-fib)](08%20Cardiology/Cardiac%20Pathologies/Arrhythmias/Ventricular%20Fibrillation%20(VF%20or%20V-fib).md)

![](00%20media/{B075DA8D-36F1-4EB6-BFB4-FCC8CB78E618}.png)

- Sustained VT
	- >30 seconds
	- Syncope
	- Requires cardioversion or antiarrhythmic drugs
- Non-sustained VT
	- Self-terminating
- Both associated with structural, valvular, and congenital heart diseases
- Wide QRS complex, 100-200 bpm ventricular rate 
- Monomorphic VT 
	- Re-entry, probably because of a pathway made by MI
	- Or ectopic ventricular focus (idiopathic VT)
- Polymorphic 
	- Torsades de pointes 
	- MI or ischemia
## How to tell the difference between VT and SVT 
![](00%20media/{085BBE99-D973-412B-BB28-235F6640B267}%201.png)

# Treatment
- Can syncope or degrade into V fib
- Use electrical cardioversion
- Amiodarone, procainamide, lidocaine when patient is stable
- Sinus rhythm restored, then try to see if there are any aggravating factors like existing heart disease 
- Implantation of an ICD considered 
- For idiopathic VT → beta blockers, Ca++ channel blockers, catheter ablation
