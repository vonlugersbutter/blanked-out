# Definition
- Persistence of normal herniation = omphalocele
- Intestines covered by membrane outside body  = “simple omphalocele”
	- Liver does not herniate
- If lateral embryonic folds fail → liver in omphalocele = liver-containing omphalocele

# Clinical features
- Herniation is covered in peritoneum, and passes through the umbilical cord, as opposed to [Gastroschisis](Gastroenterology/GI%20Disorders/Gastroschisis.md)
- Normal GI function, many associated genetic defects
	- Trisomy 21 (Down syndrome)
	- Trisomy 18 (Edwards syndrome)
	- Trisomy 13
- Congenital heart defects (up to 50% babies)
- Orofacial clefts
- Neural tube defects
