# Definition
Abnormal billowing of a portion of one or both mitral leaflets into LA during systole, usually accompanied with [Mitral Regurgitation](Cardiology/Cardiac%20Pathologies/Valvular%20Diseases/Mitral%20Regurgitation.md). 
# Etiology
- Inherited as autosomal dominant with variable penetrance (describes the likelihood or proportion of individuals with a specific disease-causing genotype who actually express the associated phenotype (observable trait))
- Connective tissue 
	- Marfan syndrome
	- Ehlers-Danlos syndrome 

- Basically, the posterior (usually) or other leaflets, are enlarged dense collagen and elastin fragmented and replaced with → myxomatous degeneration of the valve (tissue change where glycosaminoglycan accumulates, often leading to a breakdown of collagen and elastic fibers)
# Epidemiology
2% of population, usually women. 
# Prognosis
# Investigations
## Echocardiogram
- Posterior displacement of one or more leaflets during systole
## EKG
- Normal, unless chronic MR led to LA and LV enlargement
## CXR
- Normal, unless chronic MR led to LA and LV enlargement
# Clinical features
- Often asymptomatic
- Chest pain or palpitation because of arrythmia 
- Midsystolic click = sudden tensing of the mitral leaflet or chordae tendineae when it bows back 
- Late systolic murmur best heard at apex = regurgitant flow through the incompetent valve
- When LV volume increased (squatting) → more tension on chordae tendineae, limit and delay occurrence of prolapse 
	- Opposite occurs for decreasing LV volume, like when you suddenly stand 
# Treatments
- Benign 
- Monitor for [Mitral Regurgitation](Cardiology/Cardiac%20Pathologies/Valvular%20Diseases/Mitral%20Regurgitation.md) complications 
- Rupture of the chordae can cause severe [Mitral Regurgitation](Cardiology/Cardiac%20Pathologies/Valvular%20Diseases/Mitral%20Regurgitation.md) and pulmonary edema
- Watch for complications, like [Infective Endocarditis](Cardiology/Cardiac%20Pathologies/Valvular%20Diseases/Infective%20Endocarditis.md), peripheral emboli due to microthrombus formation, arrythmias
