# Definition
Insufficient PTH.
# Etiology
- Characterized by low PTH and low calcium, often due to accidental surgical excision or autoimmune conditions like autoimmune polyendocrine syndrome type 1 (APS-1)
- DiGeorge syndrome (thymic aplasia) also causes hypoparathyroidism due to the failure of the 3rd and 4th pharyngeal pouches to form

# Clinical features
- Symptoms include tetany, Chvostek's sign, and Trousseau's sign
	- Chvostek =  abnormal twitching of muscles that are activated (innervated) by the facial nerve → ipsilateral facial spasm when tapping in front of ear
	- Trousseau's = a blood pressure cuff is placed around the arm and inflated to a pressure greater than the systolic BP →  occlude the brachial artery
		- Patient's hypocalcemia and subsequent neuromuscular irritability will induce spasm of the muscles of the hand and forearm
