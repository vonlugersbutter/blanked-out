# Gametogenesis
- Development of haploid gametes
- Primordial germ cells = common origins of spermatozoa and oocytes, derived from epiblast cells
- Migrate to reside among endoderm cells of yolk sac
- During 8th week: migrate to genital ridge

![](00%20media/Pasted%20image%2020260311221416.png)
## Ovum
- Primordial germ cells → oogonia (2n 2C)
- Oogonia divide in utero = 1° oocytes (2n 4C)
- Maximum number formed by 5th month in utero
- Surrounded by cells → primordial follicle
- Primary oocytes: diploid cells formed in utero, beginning meiosis I, arrested in prophase of meiosis I until puberty
- At puberty, menstrual cycles begin, a few primary oocytes complete meiosis 1 each cycle
- Some form polar bodies and degenerate, some form 2° oocytes
- 2° oocytes (1n 2C)
- Meiosis II begins → arrests in metaphase
- No fertilization: oocyte degenerates → see [Menstrual Cycle](14%20Endocrinology/Menstrual%20Cycle.md)
- Fertilization → completion of meiosis II, forms ovum (1n 1C)
- Arrested in the metaphase of the second meiotic division, surrounded by zona pellucida 
- Midcycle LH surge allows corona radiata cells to disperse

## Sperm
- Begins at puberty = sex cords in testes develop a lumen
- Begin with spermatogonia = 2N2C, from primordial germ cells, precursors of spermatozoa
- Undergo mitosis → becomes one spermatogonium and one 1o spermatocyte
- 1o spermatocyte = 2N4C, DNA synthesis completed, starting meiosis I
- 2o spermatocyte = 1N2C, meiosis I completed, starting meiosis II
- Spermatids = haploid (1n 1C) = undergo spermiogenesis to form spermatozoa (sperm)
- Spermiogenesis = formation of spermatozoa
	- Formation of acrosome = cap of sperm, contains enzymes to assist in fertilization
	- Condensation of nucleus, formation of neck and tail, shedding of most of cytoplasm
- Sertoli Cells = line walls of seminiferous tubules, support and nourish developing spermatozoa, regulate spermatogenesis
	- Stimulated by FSH, supported by Leydig cell testosterone (paracrine)
	- Need FSH and LH for normal spermatogenesis
	- Form blood-testis barrier = tight junctions between adjacent Sertoli cells, isolates sperm; protection from autoimmune attack
		- Apical side (toward tubule): meiosis, spermiogenesis
		- Basal side: spermatogonia cell division
- Seminiferous tubules contain = spermatocyte, spermatids spermatogonia, Sertoli cells, Leydig cells
- Capacitation = glycoprotein coat covering the spermatozoa is removed, reorganization of the membrane, change from undulating wave movements to whip-like thrashing 
	- Ca++ sensitivity ↑, cAMP ↑ 
- Acrosome reaction = ZP3 binding, more Ca++ influx, acrosome swells, released onto head of the sperm, proteolytic enzymes allow for penetration of the zona pellucida
