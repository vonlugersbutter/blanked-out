# Definition
Each great vessel comes from the wrong ventricle. 
![200](00%20media/{327E2416-D338-4C11-A1AA-20CD4905D053}.png)
# Etiology
- Failure of aorticopulmonary segment to spiral properly 
- Could be failure of the infundibuli to grow
- Blood from venous system goes right back to system
- Extremely hypoxic, cyanotic baby
- Compatible with life in utero because flow through ductus arteriosus and foramen ovale allows for communication 
	- Umbilical vein → RA → LA through foramen ovale → LV → pulmonary artery → ductus arteriosus → aorta 
- If the ductus arteriosus and foramen ovale remain patent, communication is maintained, and can try to have adequate perfusion
