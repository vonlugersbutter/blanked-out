Most deadly arrythmia. Ventricular fiber contraction is asynchronous. 

![](00%20media/{79E12E81-171C-4B68-8002-902443B8E568}.png)
# Etiology
Associated with inadequate blood flow to the heart due to coronary artery disease, like during MI. Or cardiovascular shock, electrical shock, drowning, hypo (VERY HYPO)kalemia. Major cause of mortality in acute MI. 

Thought to be breaking up of excitation waves into smaller re-entry waves, wander through the myocardium. 
# Prognosis
Seizures and irreversible brain damage if not treated after five minutes.
# Treatment
- CPR
- Defibrillation = strong, brief electrical current that can help stop it, using a defibrillator 
- Automatic implantable cardioverter defibrillator (AICD)
