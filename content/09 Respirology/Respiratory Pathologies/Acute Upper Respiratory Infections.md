# Definition
Infections at or above the vocal chords, so everything down to the larynx. Commons ones include common cold, acute rhinosinusitis, pharyngitis, laryngitis, influenza, croup, COVID-19. 
# Etiology
Note that a lot of these upper respiratory tract infection pathogens can also cause trouble for the lower respiratory tract. 

- Viral pathogens (more common)
	- Rhinovirus
	- Coronavirus
	- Influenza virus
	- Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)
	- Adenovirus
	- Parainfluenza virus
	- Human metapneumovirus
	- Epstein-Barr virus
	- Enterovirus
- Bacterial pathogens
	- *Streptococcus pneumoniae*
	- *Streptococcus pyogenes*
	- *Haemophilus influenzae*
	- *Mycoplasma pneumoniae*
	- *Moraxella catarrhalis*

# Epidemiology
More common in fall and winter, probably because of close contact with others. Usually happens more in children than adults, likely because of developing immune systems and less hygienic practices.
