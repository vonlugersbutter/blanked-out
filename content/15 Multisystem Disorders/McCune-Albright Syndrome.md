# Definition
Rare disorder that affects many endocrine organs. 

# Etiology
- Somatic activating mutation in GNAS (Gs-alpha), sporadic mutation in development (not inherited)
- GNAS gene → alpha subunit of G3 protein, activates adenylyl cyclase, ↑ cAMP
- Postzygotic mutation
- Leads to mosaicism with fibrous dysplasia
- Germline occurences are lethal 
# Epidemiology

# Clinical features
- Precocious puberty despite GH excess, menstruation as early as 2
- Fibrous dysplasia → growth in bones, fractures, deformities
- Skin pigmentation
	- Café au lait spots, irregular borders (Coast of Maine)
