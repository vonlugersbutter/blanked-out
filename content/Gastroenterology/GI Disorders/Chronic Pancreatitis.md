# Definition
Involves the permanent fibrosis and calcification of the pancreas due to recurrent bouts of acute inflammation.
# Etiology
- In adults, this is most commonly caused by alcohol; in children, it is often due to cystic fibrosis

# Epidemiology

# Clinical features
- Chronic abdominal pain that may be worse after meals, leading to weight loss and "fear of eating"
- Amylase and lipase levels may be normal or only mildly elevated because the fibrosis reduces enzyme production
# Investigations

# Prognosis
- Splenic vein thrombosis (leading to gastric varices and an enlarged spleen)
- [Diabetes](Endocrinology/Endocrine%20Pathologies/Diabetes.md)
- Fat malabsorption (steatorrhea) with vitamin deficiencies
