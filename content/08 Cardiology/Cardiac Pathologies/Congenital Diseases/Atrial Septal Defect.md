# Definition
Atrial septal defect, congenital heart issue. Foramen ovale fails to close. Can be associated with [Platypnea-orthodeoxia Syndrome](09%20Respirology/Respiratory%20Pathologies/Platypnea-orthodeoxia%20Syndrome.md). 
# Etiology
![200](00%20media/{3FA0A560-3FE8-4E80-96FA-C186CD72B42E}.png)
- Usually ostium secundum
- Could also be an ostium primum defect
- Sinus venosus defect = no tissue between right pulmonary vein and right atrium, closely related to ASD 
- Patent foramen ovale is in 20% of population, and is not a true defect, it's just tissue that remained open 
	- Remains functionally closed because left is higher than right pressure, but can get open if right side pressures are higher 
	- Could be involved in [Platypnea-orthodeoxia Syndrome](09%20Respirology/Respiratory%20Pathologies/Platypnea-orthodeoxia%20Syndrome.md)
	- Could also be implicated in paradoxical embolism 
- Oxygenated blood shunted from left to right
- RV becomes more compliant
- Volume overload on the right side, could have pulmonary overload, and pulmonary vascular disease [Eisenmenger Syndrome](08%20Cardiology/Cardiac%20Pathologies/Congenital%20Diseases/Eisenmenger%20Syndrome.md)

- 1/1500 births

# Investigations
## Physical exam
- Systolic impulse on LLSV, contraction of dilated RV (RV heave )
- Widened fixed splitting of S2 → normal changes in blood volume equaled out using ASD 
- More blood across pulmonary valve might have murmur on 2nd left intercostal 
- Increased flow across tricuspid would be LLSB murmur
- No huge pressure gradient between atria = no murmur as blood goes through ASD
## CXR
- RA and RV dilatation, large heart
## EKG
- RV hypertrophy
- RA enlargement
- Left axis deviation and RBBB
## Echo
- Can see the flow between atria
## Catheterization
- May need this to confirm ASD, and check for pressures 
# Clinical features
- Asymptomatic
- Could be murmur, but is subtle
- Dyspnea on exertion, fatigue, lower respiratory tract infection
- Atrial tachyarrhythmias due to RA enlargement 
# Treatment 
- Elective surgical repair
- Pericardial or synthetic patch
