# Protective functions

![500](00%20media/Pasted%20image%2020260312034543.png)

- Must maintain a dual-purpose mucosal interface
	- Specialized for the implantation and development of a semi-allogeneic fetus
	- Remain robust enough to eliminate pathogens (which are significant contributors to infertility and pregnancy failure) while simultaneously allowing for the growth of beneficial commensal microbes in the lower tract and the immunologically distinct fetus in the upper tract
- Lower FRT (Vagina and Ectocervix) = Characterized by non-keratinized stratified squamous epithelium and a rich commensal flora
	- Epithelial cells contain glycogen → Lactobacillus makes lactic acid → keeps the pH acidic
- Upper FRT (Uterus and Endocervix) = Lined by a single layer of columnar epithelium
	- Traditionally considered sterile, though current research suggests it may harbor a small commensal population
- Normal Vaginal Environment = Characterized by a white or clear discharge with a physiological pH between 3.8 and 4.2
- Physical/Chemical = The stratified epithelium acts as a structural barrier, supported by mucus and antimicrobial peptides (AMPs)
	- Epithelial cells and underlying fibroblasts also store and deliver antiretroviral drugs, which may aid in HIV containment
- Biological (microbiome) = dominated by Lactobacillus species, which produce lactic acid to maintain an acidic pH, secrete bacteriostatic compounds, and compete with pathogens for epithelial attachment 
- Dysbiosis = specifically a microbiome characterized by high levels of strict anaerobes (eg, Prevotella, Gardnerella), associated with increased vaginal inflammation and a higher susceptibility to HIV-1 infection → see [Sexually Transmitted Infections](13%20Reproduction/Reproductive%20Pathologies/Sexually%20Transmitted%20Infections.md)
- APCs = comprising 10–50% of leucocytes, these include intraepithelial Langerhans cells (LCs), lamina propria (LP) CD14⁻ dendritic cells (DCs), CD14⁺ DCs, and macrophages 
- Innate and Innate-like Lymphocytes 
	- NK Cells = Resemble blood NK cells and are critical for limiting viral infections (eg, HPV, HSV)
	- $\gamma\delta$ T-cells & MAIT Cells = produce IL-17 and IL-22 in response to stimuli like E coli, serving as acute sensors of tissue perturbation and bacterial infection
- Classical Adaptive Immunity = T-cells comprise 35–50% of leucocytes 
	- Unlike most mucosal sites where IgA dominates, IgG is the predominant antibody isotype in the lower FRT 
	- Persistent HSV-2 infection can trigger the formation of localized lymphoid clusters (aggregates of T-cells, B-cells, and APCs) that provide lasting local protection
- Window of susceptibility = discovery that immune responses fluctuate over the menstrual cycle suggests specific periods where women may be more vulnerable to STIs, which could impact vaccination strategies
## Maternal-fetal interfaces
- See [Placenta](13%20Reproduction/Placenta.md)
- Villous Trophoblast (VT) = Bathed in maternal blood; these cells lack MHC expression to avoid T-cell recognition
- Extravillous Trophoblast (EVT) in Spiral Arteries = EVTs transform uterine spiral arteries to ensure low-pressure blood flow to the placenta
- Interstitial EVT in the Decidua = EVTs interface with specialized decidual immune cells They lack HLA-A and -B but express HLA-C, -E, and -G to modulate NK cell and T-cell responses
- Innate Lymphoid Cells (ILCs) = Uterine NK (uNK) cells are sparse before ovulation but increase rapidly during the secretory phase, eventually accounting for ~70% of immune cells at the time of implantation and during the first trimester
	- Unlike cytotoxic blood NK cells, uNK cells are largely non-cytotoxic and produce pro-angiogenic factors to facilitate tissue remodeling and placentation
- Macrophages = Represent ~20% of the decidual leucocyte population
	- They are highly heterogeneous (subsets dM1 and dM2) and play critical roles in implantation, placental invasion (via IL-6 and IL-8 production), tissue repair during menstruation, and the initiation of parturition
- T-cells = While a minority in early pregnancy, T-cells account for over half of leucocytes by term
	- CD8⁺ T-cells = Exhibit reduced cytotoxicity under hormonal control but remain capable of expansion and activation if viral infections (like HCMV) occur
	- Tregs = Enriched in the decidua to promote tolerance to the placenta and assist in tissue repair
