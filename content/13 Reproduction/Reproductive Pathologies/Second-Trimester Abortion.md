# Etiology
- More diverse than first-trimester losses; includes fetal anomalies, [Placenta](13%20Reproduction/Placenta.md) causes (abruption/previa), chorioamnionitis, maternal disorders, and uterine defects
- Inevitable Abortion = Often stems from preterm prelabor rupture of membranes (PPROM) at a previable age Significant maternal complications include sepsis, hemorrhage, and abruption
- Cervical Insufficiency = Classically characterized by painless cervical dilation in the second trimester, often resulting in membrane ballooning and expulsion of an immature fetus
- Management = Cervical cerclage (McDonald or Shirodkar methods) reinforces a weak cervix Prophylactic placement is preferred between 12 and 14 weeks

# Treatment 
## First-Trimester Methods
- Surgical = Preoperative cervical ripening with hygroscopic dilators (Laminaria or Dilapan-S) or misoprostol (400 μg) is often used A 200-mg dose of doxycycline is given 1 hour before to prevent infection
- Medication = The favored regimen is 200 mg mifepristone followed by 800 μg misoprostol 24–48 hours later Misoprostol alone is also an option but has lower efficacy
## Second-Trimester Methods
- Dilation and Evacuation (D & E) = Requires wide cervical dilation; the fetus is extracted in parts using specialized instruments (eg, Sopher forceps)
- Medical Induction = Mifepristone/misoprostol regimens or PGE2 suppositories (dinoprostone) are used to induce labor High-dose oxytocin is another alternative but is generally slower than misoprostol
## Complications
- Mortality = Legally induced abortion is safe, and carrying a pregnancy to term is 14-fold riskier
- Complications = Uterine perforation and cervical laceration each occur in ≤1% of first-trimester cases Hemorrhage and infection are other infrequent risks
- Subsequent Pregnancies = Rates of infertility or ectopic pregnancy are not increased, though some studies suggest a 15-fold higher risk of preterm birth following surgical evacuation
## Postabortal Care
- Contraception = Ovulation can occur as early as 8 days post-abortion (average 3 weeks) Contraception, including intrauterine devices, can be initiated immediately
- Future Conception = For those desiring pregnancy, there is no need for delay; conceiving within 3 months of a first-trimester loss is associated with lower subsequent miscarriage rates
