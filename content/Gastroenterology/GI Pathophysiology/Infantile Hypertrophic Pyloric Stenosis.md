# Definition
Condition in young infants where the pylorus muscle (between the stomach and small intestine) thickens, creating a blockage that prevents food from passing, leading to severe, forceful (projectile) vomiting, dehydration, and poor weight gain
# Etiology
- Macrolide use increases risk
