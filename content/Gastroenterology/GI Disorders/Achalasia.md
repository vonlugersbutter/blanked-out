# Definition
Failure of the LES to relax due to a lack of nitric oxide synthase and VIP in inhibitory nerves
# Etiology
- The inability of the les to relax due to the loss of ganglion cells in Auerbach’s plexus it causes dysphagia to both solids and liquids and may cause bad breath from food accumulation diagnostics show a "bird's beak" on barium swallow and increased les tone on manometry
