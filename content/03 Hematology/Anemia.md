## Pernicious anemia
- Autoimmune destruction of parietal cells, leading to atrophic gastritis and failure of Vitamin B12 absorption due to intrinsic factor deficiency
- Auto-immune gastritis = ant parietal antibodies destroying parietal cells (duh) 
- Those with this have higher risk of other autoimmune disorders 
- This will lead to a megaloblastic anemia with B12 deficiency 
- A risk factor for gastric cancers → long-standing, ends up causing things like linitis plastica
