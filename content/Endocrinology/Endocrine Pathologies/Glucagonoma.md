# Definition
A tumor causing excess glucagon, leading to glucose intolerance, weight loss, and a characteristic red, blistering rash called necrolytic migratory erythema. 
# Etiology
- Functional neuroendocrine tumor derived from pancreatic α-cells
- Glucagon production ↑ 
- Chronic diarrhea, weight loss, neuropsychiatric symptoms (e.g., depression, dementia, ataxia), deep vein thrombosis, normocytic anemia (due to anemia of chronic disease and the inhibitory effect of glucagon on erythropoiesis), and necrolytic migratory erythema (shown below)
