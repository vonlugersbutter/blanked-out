# Definition
- Small bowel obstruction (SBO) = 75% of obstructions occur in the small intestine, commonly caused by adhesions (post-surgical scar tissue), hernias ("bulges"), or cancer
- Intussusception = the "telescoping" of the intestine into its own lumen, common in children <1 year old it presents with "currant jelly" stools and requires emergency treatment
	- Lead points can include [Meckel's Diverticulum](Gastroenterology/GI%20Pathologies/Meckel's%20Diverticulum.md) or lymphoid hyperplasia
- Volvulus = the twisting of the bowel around its mesentery, classically at the sigmoid colon or cecum, causing obstruction and infarction
