Thank you, Tortora, for this diagram on stuff that causes the thirst response. 

![](00%20media/{9B8732BD-E1A1-40E3-A9A5-04C62B9176EE}.png)

- Phosphate buffer system is really important in the cytosol, bicarbonate system is more important in the plasma
- The only way to get rid of acids other than carbonic acid is through the urine
- Intercalated cells have proton pumps (H+ ATPases) 
- One type of intercalated cells are Cl-/HCO3- antiporters
- Another type has proton pumps in its basolateral surface and the antiporters on its apical side, so it allows the cells to secrete HCO3- and reabsorb H+, if the blood is getting too alkaline
