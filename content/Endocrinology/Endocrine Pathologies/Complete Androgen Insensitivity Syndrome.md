# Definition
- A mutation in the androgen receptor in 46 XY males, inherited X-linked disorder 
- No internal or external male genitalia, but because MIH is present, they also lack a uterus and fallopian tubes at puberty
- Develop breasts (due to aromatization of testosterone) but have no pubic or axillary hair

# Clinical features
- Breast development, but no milk production → because no progesterone

- No cellular response to androgens 
- MIH → degeneration of Mullerian structures, so no uterus or Fallopian tubes
