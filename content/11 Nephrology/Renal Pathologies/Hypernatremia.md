# Definition
- Associated with hyperosmolarity 
- Usually because of unreplaced water losses 
- Thirst provides the ultimate protection against hypernatremia 
- Even people with diabetes insipidus are okay because they're drinking enough water to compensate
# Etiology
- Hyponatremia due to water loss will usually happen when thirst cannot be normally expressed 

- Increased water losses due to impairment of thirst
	- Insensible and sweat losses
	- Urinary losses → diabetes insipidus, osmotic diuresis due to glucose or mannitol (could also happen with diabetes mellitus!)
	- GI losses
	- Hypothalamic lesion affecting thirst centre
