# Definition
Severe pulmonary valve obstruction that results from chronic left to right shunting through cardiac defect. Elevated pulmonary resistance causes reversal of shunt, systemic cyanosis. 
# Etiology
- Pulmonary artery media hypertrophies
- Intima proliferates
- Vessels become thrombosed, higher resistance

# Investigations
## Physical exam
- Cyanotic
- Digital clubbing
- Prominent a wave
- Loud P2 
## CXR
- Pulmonary artery dilation, calcification of pulmonary vasculature
## EKG
- RV hypertrophy, RA enlargement
## Echo
- Identify the issue 
# Clinical features
- Hypoxia
- Exertional dyspnea, fatigue 
- Erythrocytosis, hyper-viscosity of blood
	- Fatigue, headaches, stroke 
- Hemoptysis 
# Treatment 
- Avoid things that exacerbate the left to right shunt 
- Prompt treatment of infections, manage disturbances
- Pulmonary vasodilator therapy 
- Try to close the shunt or treat the original issue
