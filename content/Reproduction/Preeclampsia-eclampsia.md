# Definition
- Preeclampsia = hypertension in pregnancy, proteinuria, end-organ damage
- Eclampsia = generalized tonic-clonic seizures in those with preeclampsia, probably because of abnormal blood flow 
# Etiology

![](00%20media/Pasted%20image%2020260311232209.png)

- Disorder of the [Placenta](Reproduction/Placenta.md), normally trophoblast invades/transforms spiral arteries
- Abnormal invasion/transformation → preeclampsia
- Placental under-perfusion, leads to release of circulating substances
- Diffuse maternal endothelial dysfunction, vasospasm and coagulation
- Resolves with delivery (placental removal)
- Extravillous trophoblast fails to penetrate myometrium, spiral arteries do not develop normally
- Remain narrow → placental hypoperfusion, problem develops in 3rd trimester 
- Risk factors = first pregnancy, family history, multiple gestations (twins)
	- Prior conditions like [Diabetes](Endocrinology/Endocrine%20Pathologies/Diabetes/Diabetes.md) and [Obesity](Gastroenterology/GI%20Pathologies/Obesity.md) also become risky 
# Epidemiology

# Clinical features
- Usually occurs 3rd trimester
- New onset hypertension in mother with no known HTN in first pregnancy
- Proteinuria or end-organ damage
	- Renal failure (vasospasm of renal vessels)
	- CNS (headache, visual changes, confusion)
	- Liver failure = edema, ischemia and necrosis, check LFTs 
- Proteinuria → lower oncotic pressure → edema (but this can occur in pregnancy for other reasons)
# Investigations
- Placental biopsy: fibrinoid necrosis of vessels, because of ischemia 
# Prognosis
- Placental insufficiency = growth restriction, oligohydramnios
- Placental abruption 
- Edema, heart failure, liver hematoma, failure, disseminated intravascular coagulation, stroke, dialysis 
- HELLP syndrome = variant of preeclampsia, more severe
	- Hemolysis, elevated LFTs, ↓ platelet count, coagulation activation and liver infarction 
	- Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia = schistocytes, ↑ bilirubin, ↓ haptoglobin, thrombocytopenia
# Treatment 
- Magnesium sulfate for prevention of seizures in eclampsia
- Definitive treatment is delivery
